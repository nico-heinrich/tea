Tea Explorer ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we process and why.

## What we process

- **Search terms** — Your search queries are sent to our server to retrieve matching teas. We log aggregate, anonymized search statistics to improve relevance. Individual search terms are not linked to your identity.
- **IP address** — We compute a **salted hash** of your IP and store only the **country and city** derived from it. The raw IP is never stored or transmitted to third parties.
- **Currency preference** — Your selected display currency (EUR/USD) is stored in your browser's local storage to remember your choice.
- **Recent searches** — If you opt in, your recent searches are stored in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device.

## How we use your data

We use the data above solely to provide and improve the search experience. We do **not** sell, share, or transmit personal data to third parties for advertising or tracking purposes.

## Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

- **Access** — Request information about the data we hold about you.
- **Rectification** — Request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Erasure** — Request deletion of your data.
- **Object** — Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
- **Lodge a complaint** — File a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## Cookies and local storage

We do not use tracking cookies. Local storage is used only for optional features (currency preference, recent searches) and requires your consent where applicable.
